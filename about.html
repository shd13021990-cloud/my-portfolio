✦ THE STRATEGIST BEHIND THE SCREENS

Craft, Digital Discipline & Personal Bio

Founder of Zotir Systems. Growth strategist, visual narrative designer, and digital craftsman with a 14-year professional track record spanning GCC operations, database precision, and modern social media systems.

PERSONAL NARRATIVE & JOURNEY

From GCC Site Operations to Digital Craftsmanship

I didn't start in digital marketing through viral trends — I started in high-stakes operational environments. Beginning in 2009 across Saudi Arabia and the UAE, I led commercial facility sites, civil works crews, and logistics distribution for multinational projects. Operating under intense GCC standards instilled in me a non-negotiable discipline: zero margin for error, strict timeline adherence, and deep accountability.

Later, I spent seven years at IVCC Engineering in Lahore, advancing from Data Entry Operator to Inventory Control Officer. Managing enterprise ERP databases and tracking tens of thousands of asset records shaped my analytical approach. When I pursued my BS in Mass Communication at the Virtual University of Pakistan, everything converged: my structural data rigor combined with high-impact communication theory and narrative psychology.

Japanese Digital Craftsmanship & Aesthetic Restraint

Inspired by the ethos of Japanese craftsmanship (Monozukuri) and minimalist Swiss design, I treat digital growth as an engineering discipline. Every social hook, layout proportion, typography choice, and ad copy variation is tested and refined for measurable ROI, not vanity metrics.

Today, as the founder of Zotir Systems and Social Media & Sales Manager for The Rockets Consulting (Dubai), I partner with forward-thinking founders, e-commerce brands, and global agencies to scale attention into community and community into predictable revenue.
